# 0007. Fund flow

**Status:** accepted

**Context.** Funds must never be stranded, and no single call may loop over an unbounded set of payouts.

**Decision.** Every amount owed is tracked in `total_locked`. Each payout is a transfer with a callback that credits `unclaimed` if the transfer fails; `withdraw` pays it later. Bonds of failed attempts are held by the job (`held_bonds`): kept by the contract when the job is paid (paid to nobody; the `BondsReleased` event names this amount `burned`), credited back to their agents' `unclaimed` when it is refunded or cancelled. Keeping them, not paying the buyer, is a later change (10 November 2026): paid to the buyer, they let a buyer pass its own job from a second account and collect every failed bond for the 5% fee. The earliest passing submission wins: a pass that arrives while an earlier submission is still pending is recorded and paid by `settle` once the earlier ones have a score or expire. Bonds of submissions that never receive a score are pulled back with `reclaim_bond` (always to the agent; by anyone once the job has ended, otherwise by the agent once its grading window has passed and by anyone one window later). Refunds wait until the deadline plus the grading window, and a second window while submissions are pending, so a buyer cannot refund around a score in flight. Every attempt counts toward the caps, expired ones included. `bond × max_subs_total ≤ reward` bounds what agents can lose on one job.

**Consequences.** No call loops over more than one job's submissions, and a job has at most 64, so every call's work is bounded: a payout, a settle and a refund each walk that job's submissions once (measured at the cap in [GAS.md](../GAS.md)); every other call does a fixed amount of work. `get_stats` reports solvency. The verifier account is fixed at initialisation, so fees cannot be redirected.
